# BY JHOVE! Explain Yourself

## Purpose of the Working Group

The **JHOVE SIG** was established to support users of JHOVE who encounter challenges with:

- Validation errors and warnings
- Interpreting output reports
- Inconsistent or unclear documentation
- Onboarding and user-friendliness
- Bug identification and reporting
- Collaborative improvement of the tool

The group is community-oriented and welcomes both technical and non-technical participants. **Development expertise is not required.**

### Primary Focus Areas

The group's current focus areas include:

- Errors and warnings interpretation
- Documentation improvements
- User-friendliness and onboarding
- Community knowledge sharing
- Identifying software bugs and enhancement opportunities

## Meetings and Participation

The group is **open to everyone** and aims to be inclusive of participants with all levels of experience and technical ability.

### Meetings Currently

- Occur **monthly**
- Are held **bi-monthly for each timezone**
- Are typically held on the **second Tuesday of each month**
- Take place at **14:00 CET** for the European timezone

### Suggested Expectations for Participants

Participants are encouraged to:

- Attend meetings where possible
- Share problematic files or outputs
- Contribute examples and use cases
- Review documentation drafts
- Test fixes and releases
- Identify ambiguities in error messaging
